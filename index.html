Appointments, cars and service history for your garage

A free Windows app for car garages. Book work in the calendar, fill in job cards, print them for the customer, and find any car's history in a second. It works offline and keeps your data on your own PC.

Formerly called GarageManager. Already using it? Click Download in the app's “new version” bar and run the installer: it replaces GarageManager with GearBear and keeps all your data.

Everything the front desk needs, nothing it doesn't

Made together with a working garage, for receptionists and mechanics who use it all day.

Calendar

Day and week views per bay and mechanic. Drag a booking to move it. Clear colours show what's booked, in progress, waiting for parts or ready.

Job cards

Labour, parts and other costs with VAT worked out for you. Print a job card for the workshop and a summary for the customer.

Instant search

Type part of a plate, VIN, name or phone number and the car is there, even with 100 000 vehicles. Ctrl+K from anywhere.

History and reminders

Every job on every car, with mileage. See which cars are due for a service by date or kilometres, and mark who you've called.

Parts storage

Keep track of the parts and fluids on your shelves. Take them straight onto a job card and the stock updates itself.

Statistics

Jobs, turnover, labour and parts for any period. See which part makes last longest, in kilometres, on real cars.

Daily backups

Backs itself up every day, to another drive if you like. A power cut can't damage the data. Restoring takes two clicks.

Private and offline

No account, no cloud, no monthly fee. Your customers' data never leaves your computer. It runs well on old laptops too.

A look inside

Light and dark, following your Windows setting. Screenshots show made-up sample data.

Download and install

For Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The installer takes about a minute.

Installer (recommended) Portable exe, no install

  1. Download the installer

    Click the button above. The file is called GearBear-Setup.exe.

  2. Allow it to run

    Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” window. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Why? Windows shows this for programs from small publishers without a paid code-signing certificate. It is not a sign of a virus, and it only appears the first time.

  3. Install

    Click Next and Install. You get a shortcut on the desktop and in the Start menu. If the PC lacks Microsoft WebView2, the installer adds it.

  4. Set it up

    Open Settings: enter your garage name and details for printouts, opening hours, VAT, mechanics and bays. Choose a backup folder on another drive. Then book your first appointment.

Updates

When a new version is out, a bar appears at the top of the app. Click Download, close GearBear and run the new installer. All your data stays.

Already have customers in Excel?

Save the sheet as CSV and import it in Settings → Import. You choose which column is which.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, for as long as you use it. There's no trial and no locked features. Donations are welcome but never required.

Where is my data, and does anything get sent anywhere?

Everything is in one database file in %APPDATA%\GearBear on your PC, plus the backups. Settings → About shows the exact folder. The only internet use is a check for new versions: the app reads one small file from this website. No data about you, your garage or your customers is sent, and you can switch the check off in Settings.

Can several PCs share the same data?

Not yet. GearBear runs on one PC, usually at the front desk. To move to a new PC, make a backup on the old one and restore it on the new one.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

No, only on Windows 10 and 11.

What is the portable exe for?

It's the same app without an installer. Put it in a folder such as C:\GearBear and run it. To keep the data in that folder too, for example on a USB stick, create an empty file named portable next to the exe before the first start.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → GearBear → Uninstall. Your database and backups are kept in case you change your mind. Delete the %APPDATA%\GearBear folder to remove them as well.
