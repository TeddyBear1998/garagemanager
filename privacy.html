Privacy

What GearBear and this website do with your data: very little.

Last updated: 04.11.2026

The app: your garage's data stays on your PC

GearBear has no accounts and no cloud. Customers, vehicles, appointments, job cards and backups are stored only on the computer where it runs (or in its own folder, when you use the portable version). ImpatientBeastStudios never receives them.

The app's only network access is the update check: at start and every 12 hours it downloads one small file, latest.json, from this website to see whether a newer version exists. It sends nothing about you or your garage. You can switch it off in Settings → General → Updates. When you choose Update now, the new version is downloaded from GitHub and checked against its checksum before it is installed.

The app never shows ads.

This website

The website has no accounts and no forms, and it does not set cookies of its own. Your language choice is saved in your browser so the site opens in the same language next time. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. Installers are downloaded from GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.

Advertising on the website (Google AdSense)

GearBear is free. To help cover its costs, the website shows ads from Google AdSense: one after the list of features and one at the bottom of the page.

Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, to measure them and, if you agree, to personalise them based on your visits to this and other websites. Visitors in the EU, EEA and UK are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads or such cookies are used, and can change their choice at any time with the privacy settings link that Google shows on the page.

How Google uses data from sites that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings.

Who runs GearBear

GearBear is made by ImpatientBeastStudios. Questions about privacy: info@impatientbeaststudios.com. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
